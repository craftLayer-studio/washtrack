## Trends 2024–2026

1. **Franchising formalises the category.** Tumbledry (1,500+ stores) and UClean (towards 1,000) train customers to expect tags, updates and pickup.
2. **WhatsApp as the customer channel.** Per-message pricing since 1 July 2025; utility templates inside the service window charged from 1 October 2026; INR billing mandatory by 31 December 2026 ([Meta](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)).
3. **Subscription laundry** (monthly kg plans) spreads in metros for working professionals and PG residents (estimate based on chain offers; verify in pilot).
4. **Shoe and bag care** grows as a premium add-on with before/after content for social media (estimate).
