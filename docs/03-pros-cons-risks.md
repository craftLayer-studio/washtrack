# WashTrack pros, cons and risks

## Pros

| Pro | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Pain is concrete and costly: lost or damaged garments lead to compensation and consumer-court cases (docs/04) | Clear ROI story |
| Higher willingness to pay than kiranas; Indian laundry software already sells at ₹500–2,499/month (docs/04) | ARPU ~₹1,100 vs ShelfMate ~₹450 |
| Daily, high-frequency use (every intake, every scan) | Sticky; low churn once tags are in use |
| Consumables (tag rolls) give recurring add-on revenue | ARPU |
| Organised chains (Tumbledry 1,500+ stores) prove customers value branded experience | Independents want to look as professional |
| Reuses core CRM, messaging, payments, QR | MVP in 6–8 weeks |

## Cons

| Con | Impact |
|---|---|
| Smaller number of target businesses than kirana | Fewer prospects per city; higher CAC |
| Hardware dependency (label printer, tags) | Setup friction, support load |
| Incumbent QDC is well known and franchise brands mandate their own software | Must win independents on price and WhatsApp-first UX |
| Counter speed matters; photos add seconds | Staff may skip photos |
| Many very small press shops (istriwala) cannot pay | Must filter ICP carefully |

## Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tags do not survive dry-cleaning solvent or ironing | Medium | High | Test 3 tag types before launch; offer reusable pin tags for bags/shoes; QR printed on cover bag as fallback |
| Staff skip intake photos | High | Medium | Photo required only for flagged/premium items; one-tap burst capture; owner report of photo coverage |
| WhatsApp utility cost rises (utility templates in service window charged from 1 Oct 2026) | High | Medium | 2 templates per order by default; let customers reply to open service window; quotas per plan; wallet for overage |
| WhatsApp account quality drop from marketing | Medium | High | Opt-in only, frequency caps, STOP keyword |
| DPDP: addresses, phone numbers and garment photos are personal data | Medium | High | Consent per purpose, retention limit on photos (for example 90 days after delivery unless disputed), deletion on request, processor terms with store |
| Liability policy seen as unfair under consumer law | Medium | Medium | Template policy reviewed by a lawyer; declared-value option; WashTrack evidence log helps, does not replace fair settlement |
| QDC cuts price or bundles WhatsApp | Medium | Medium | Compete on phone-first UX, onboarding service, pickups and monthly plans |
| Low order volume stores churn | Medium | Medium | ICP filter: 300+ orders/month; Starter cap and price |

## Kill criteria

| Signal | Threshold | Checkpoint |
|---|---|---|
| Trial to paid | under 20% of activated trials | Week 12 after launch |
| Usage depth | under 50% of paid stores tag 80%+ of items | Month 3 |
| Churn | paid logo churn above 6% per month for 3 months | Month 6 |
| CAC payback | above 10 months on gross margin | Month 6 |
| Sales velocity | under 8 paid stores per rep per month after ramp | Month 4 |

If tagging adoption fails but WhatsApp updates are valued, pivot to a lighter "order status + payment link" plan at ₹299/month for press shops and small laundries.
