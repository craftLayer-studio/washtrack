# WashTrack go-to-market and onboarding

## Strategy in one line

Sell to busy independent dry cleaners face to face with a live tag-and-WhatsApp demo, then grow through the equipment and chemical dealers who already visit them.

## Beachhead

| Choice | Detail |
|---|---|
| City | One Tier 1 city with many independent dry cleaners and office catchments (for example Hyderabad, Pune, Bengaluru) |
| List | 150 storefronts from Google Maps, JustDial and street walks, filtered by 4+ rating and 50+ reviews (proxy for volume) |
| Store filter | 300+ orders/month; 2+ staff; already does pickups or gets WhatsApp enquiries; not a Tumbledry/UClean franchise |
| Avoid at first | One-person press (istri) stalls; franchise outlets with mandated software |

## Channels

| Channel | How | Expected CAC (estimate) | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder / field rep demos | Walk in at slow hours (11 am–4 pm), demo tag print + WhatsApp message on owner's phone | ₹4,300 | Month 1+ |
| Equipment and chemical dealers | Steam press, ironing table, washer and solvent dealers visit laundries monthly; pay ₹1,000 per paid store referred | ₹1,500 + follow-up | Month 3+ |
| Owner referral | 1 free month per referred paid store | ₹1,300 | Month 2+ |
| Laundry owner WhatsApp/Facebook groups and trade expos (for example Clean India Show) | Case-study posts, demo videos, expo stall shared with CraftLayer | ₹2,500 | Month 4+ |
| Google Search ads for "laundry software" / "dry cleaning software" | Small budget to demo request; competitors bid here | ₹3,000–5,000 | Month 3+ |
| YouTube Shorts in Hindi/Telugu/Tamil | "How Imran stopped losing garments" | ₹1,500 | Month 4+ |
| Small multi-store brands (3–10 stores) | Direct founder sale of Plant plan | ₹8,000 per account, higher ARPU | Month 6+ |

## First 10 paying customers: playbook

1. **Week 0.** Buy 2 label printers and 3 tag types; test them through wash, dry-clean and iron. Prepare demo phone with a sample store.
2. **Week 1–2.** Visit 60 shortlisted stores. In each, print a tag for one of their garments, scan it, and send a WhatsApp "received" message to the owner's own phone. Book 20 trial set-ups.
3. **Setup visit (90 minutes).** Load price list, connect printer, train counter staff on 10 live intakes, put up a counter poster: "Ab har kapde par QR tag. WhatsApp par update milega."
4. **Day 3 and 7.** Check tag coverage and photo coverage reports; fix staff habits.
5. **Day 10–14.** Show the owner the numbers: items tagged, messages sent, UPI payments collected, calls saved (owner estimate). Close annual at ₹5,999 / ₹12,999 with free tags, or monthly. Target 10 paid from 20 trials.
6. **Day 30.** Ask for 2 referrals and a dealer introduction. Record a testimonial.

## Sales script (3 minutes)

1. **Open.** "Pichhle mahine kitne kapde idhar-udhar hue, ya kisi customer ne damage ka claim kiya?"
2. **Show.** Print a QR tag on their shirt. Take a stain photo. The owner's phone receives the WhatsApp: "Received 1 item, ready by Thursday, photo attached."
3. **Value.** "Har kapde ki photo aur scan history. Consumer court mein bhi 'no responsibility' board nahi chalta, photo chalti hai."
4. **Second hook.** "Ready hote hi customer ko apne aap message. Phone calls band, aur UPI link se paisa jaldi."
5. **Close.** "14 din free, 200 tags free. Pasand aaye toh ₹599 se shuru."

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Already using QDC / another software." | "Kya WhatsApp updates included hain? Hamare har plan mein hain. Price list hum import kar denge, switch 1 din mein." |
| "Tag lagane mein time lagega." | "Ek tag 3 second. Galat kapda dhoondhne mein 30 minute lagte hain." |
| "Staff photo nahi lega." | "Sirf mehenge ya pehle se damage wale kapdon ki photo. Report mein dikhega kaun le raha hai." |
| "Customer WhatsApp pe message nahi chahta." | "Pehle message mein hi STOP ka option hai. Zyada customers khush hote hain ki call nahi karna pada." |
| "₹1,299 zyada hai." | "Ek damage claim ₹6,000 se ₹25,000 tak ka hota hai. Ek bhi bacha toh saal ka paisa vasool. Starter ₹599 se shuru." |
| "Printer kharidna padega." | "Zaroori nahi. Pre-printed tag roll scan kar lo phone se. Printer baad mein." |

## Onboarding checklist (setup visit, 60–90 minutes)

- [ ] Business profile: name, address, hours, GST mode, express rules, due-date defaults
- [ ] Price list loaded from template; owner edits prices for top 30 items and per-kg services
- [ ] Tag method chosen: printer label / pre-printed roll / pin tags; test tag through one wash cycle
- [ ] Printer paired (if used); receipt template with liability policy and consent notice
- [ ] Workflow stages configured (received, washing/dry clean, pressing, packed, ready, delivered)
- [ ] WhatsApp sender set (CraftLayer number with store name, or store's own number via BSP); templates approved
- [ ] UPI ID linked; ₹1 test payment
- [ ] Counter staff do 10 live intakes with tags and photos
- [ ] Workshop staff scan 10 items through stages
- [ ] Existing pending orders migrated (or run paper and app in parallel for 7 days)
- [ ] Owner taught dashboard: ready-not-collected, dues, disputes
- [ ] Growth: riders added, delivery zones drawn, slot times set
- [ ] Day-3 check-in booked

## Activation and retention

| Moment | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | 10 live intakes during setup | Rep |
| Day 3 | Tag and photo coverage review | Rep |
| Day 7 | Ready-not-collected reminder run; first UPI payments shown | Rep |
| Weekly | Owner digest: items tagged, messages sent, payments collected, uncollected orders, disputes | Automated |
| Day 14 | Conversion meeting with numbers | Rep |
| Monthly | Top customers list + suggested monthly plan offer | Automated |
| Red flag | Tagging below 50% of orders for 3 days, or no logins for 2 days: call within 24 hours | Support |

Retention levers: tags and printers in daily use (switching cost), customer history and wallets held in WashTrack, monthly plans that customers prepay, annual billing, weekly digest that shows money collected.

## Referral programme

- Owner: 1 free month per referred paid store.
- Dealer: ₹1,000 per paid store after 2 paid months.
- Customer-facing: WhatsApp receipt footer "Laundry business? Try WashTrack" link.
