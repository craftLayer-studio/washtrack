# WashTrack pricing and revenue

Exchange rate: ₹88 = US$1 **(estimate)**. Prices exclude 18% GST. Competitor anchors in [04-market-research.md](04-market-research.md).

## Pricing logic

- Indian laundry software sells at ₹500–2,499/month (Dhobi Cart, Swash, QDC Pro at ~₹999/month, Laundry Grow). WashTrack Starter enters below QDC Pro with WhatsApp included.
- Growth at ₹1,299 is roughly QDC Pro plus its WhatsApp add-on, and includes pickups and monthly plans.
- One prevented compensation payout (Rs 6,000 to Rs 25,000 in reported consumer cases) pays for a year of Starter. Use this in the pitch.
- Per-store pricing with monthly order caps, like QDC and Fabklean. Caps drive upgrades.

Annual plans (₹5,999 / ₹12,999) recover CAC on day 1 for Growth and in month 1 for Starter. Lead with annual plus free setup visit.

## 12-month scenarios (estimate)
