# WashTrack problems and benefits

Format: problem, then WashTrack's solution, then the benefit and the metric it moves.

## Owner

| Problem | Solution | Benefit | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Garments lost or swapped between orders | QR tag per item; scan at each stage and at handover; mismatch alert | Fewer losses and compensation payouts | Retention |
| Damage claims ("you burnt my sherwani"); consumer commissions have held cleaners liable and rejected blanket disclaimers (see docs/04) | Intake photos with timestamp, declared value for premium items, clear liability policy on receipt | Pre-existing damage is provable; fair settlements faster | Retention |
| Staff spend hours on "is it ready?" calls | Automatic WhatsApp updates: received, ready, out for delivery | Fewer calls, fewer walk-ins to an unready order | Activation |
| Ready orders not collected for weeks; cash stuck | Reminders with UPI link; ready-not-collected list | Faster cash collection, less storage | Retention |
| Pickups missed or riders zig-zag across town | Slot booking and area-sorted route list | More pickups per rider per day | ARPU (Growth) |
| One-time customers do not come back | Monthly plans, win-back offers, referral credit | Predictable prepaid revenue | ARPU for owner, retention |
| Paper challans and manual price lookup | Price list templates, per-kg and per-piece billing, GST invoice | Faster intake, fewer billing errors | Activation |
| No idea who the best customers are | Customer history and spend ranking | Target offers at regulars | Retention |

## Counter and workshop staff

| Problem | Solution | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Handwritten tags fade or fall off | Washable QR tags | Correct item every time |
| Unclear priorities | Due-today and express list | Fewer delays |
| Blame when something goes missing | Scan log shows who handled each item | Fair accountability |

## Rider

| Problem | Solution | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Phone calls to find addresses | Route list with map link and notes | More stops per day |
| Cash mismatch | Collect amount shown; UPI on doorstep | Clean cash handover |

## End customer

| Problem | Solution | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Does not know when clothes are ready | WhatsApp update with due time | No wasted trips |
| Worried about expensive garments | Photos at intake, tag tracking, written policy | Trust |
| Has to visit the shop | Pickup and delivery booking | Convenience |
| Carrying cash | UPI link | Easy payment |
| Spam fear | Separate marketing consent, STOP opt-out | Control |

## Pitch lines

2. "Customer ko WhatsApp par apne aap pata chalega ki kapde ready hain. Phone calls band."
3. "Monthly plan bechiye, advance paisa lijiye."
